The valuation estimate

This is the most important section on this page, so it is written plainly.

The calculator produces a rough estimate based only on the answers you type in. It applies general multiples drawn from what businesses in broad sectors have sold for, adjusted for the revenue trend, the proportion of recurring revenue and the level of owner involvement you tell us about.

It does not verify anything you enter. It does not look at your accounts, your contracts, your customer concentration, your premises, your assets, your liabilities, your location or your market. It does not reflect any offer that any buyer has made or would make.

It is not a valuation. It is not advice, an appraisal, a professional opinion or a fairness opinion, and it is not suitable for any purpose that requires one. That includes tax, accounting, lending, insurance, litigation, shareholder, matrimonial and probate purposes.

You should not rely on it. What a business actually sells for depends on due diligence, the specific buyer, the deal structure and the market at the time, none of which the calculator can see. Two businesses giving identical answers could sell for very different amounts, or not sell at all.

We give no warranty that any estimate is accurate or complete, and we accept no liability for anything you do, or do not do, on the basis of one. If a number matters to you, instruct a professional to prepare a proper valuation.
