FAQs

What does "qualified" actually mean?
On our onboarding call, we agree on exactly what your criteria are for a qualified lead. We only send you owners who meet those criteria, so after the intro call you can confirm they matched your expectations. If they don't, you don't pay.
How does the pricing work?
Pricing is performance-based: you pay per intro call, once the call has taken place and you've confirmed the owner matches the brief we sent. If they don't meet your criteria, you don't pay. The price per call varies depending on the size of the business you're meeting with, and we confirm all pricing on the discovery call.
How quickly will we see results?
We always have campaigns running, so depending on your industry, you could start seeing booked calls within the first week. On the discovery call, we can give you a clearer expected timeline once we understand who you target.
How is this different from buying leads from a normal provider?
Most providers hand you a name and contact details and wish you luck, even though many aren't real sellers. We speak to every owner first, qualify them against your criteria, write you a brief, and book the call into your calendar. You only pay once you've confirmed the lead was qualified.
Will this work for our industry?
We can tailor outreach to most industries, and we currently run evergreen campaigns across trades and construction, industrial and logistics, professional services, business services, technology, and healthcare and practices. If you operate in any of these, it works. The one requirement is that the owners are US-based, since that's where we operate.
What if our industry isn't listed?
If your niche isn't one we're already running, we can usually build outreach for it, as long as the owners are US-based businesses. The quickest way to find out is a short discovery call: tell us the industries you cover and we'll confirm what we can source.
