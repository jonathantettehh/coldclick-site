Pricing

Pay per qualified intro call that takes place

No retainer, no setup fee, no percentage of any deal you close. You only pay for the intro calls that happen and match the criteria we agree with you.

How this works
Step one

You tell us what qualified means

On the onboarding call we agree your criteria in writing. Every call after that is judged against these four things and nothing else.

Revenue
The size of business you want. Most advisors work somewhere between $5M and $50M in revenue.
Location
US-wide by default. If you only want owners from certain regions or states, we focus there.
Industry
We focus on the sectors you take mandates in, and exclude any you would rather not see.
Timeline
We agree a suitable window on the onboarding call, and every owner we book fits it.
Step two

We find the owners and book them into your calendar

Everything between agreeing your criteria and the call starting is our job.

We source
US business owners in your industries who are open to selling.
We qualify
A real conversation against your four criteria before anything reaches you.
We book
Straight into your calendar, at a time that works for you.
They turn up
Chasing and rebooking is our job. You just run the intro call.
Step three

You only pay for the qualified intro calls that take place

Three things that cost you nothing. We either keep following up until the timing is right, or take them off your list.

No-shows
The owner doesn't turn up. It doesn't count and it doesn't cost you. We chase them and rebook.
Reschedules
Moving a call costs you nothing. We only charge for calls that take place.
Owners who don't match
They turn up but miss the criteria we agreed. It doesn't count and it doesn't cost you.
